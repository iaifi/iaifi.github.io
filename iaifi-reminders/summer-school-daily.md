## IAIFI Summer School Day 5
Welcome to the fifth and final day of the IAIFI Summer School 2026! Follow along with today's schedule below.

The lightning talks, hackathon presentations, and close-out presentation will be held in lecture hall Y02-2120 in University Hall and on the same Zoom link for those attending virtually. The sessions will also be recorded and the recordings will be shared at the end of the day. 

Most of the day will be dedicated to hackathon work, per the schedule below.  

Make sure to [sign up your hackathon team](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSet8dT2C8BQ4-VUxBxkaFu3qnl1PfUEychhENRS1Gn-1wiJcA/viewform) **by noon today!**
