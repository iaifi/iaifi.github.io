### RSVP: Summer School Social (Thursday, August 6), Industry Day (Friday, August 14)

On Thursday, August 6, 2026, all IAIFI members are invited to the 2026 IAIFI Summer School Social at the Museum of Science. The event will include a catered dinner at the Museum hosted by one of our sponsors, PDT Partners, with remarks from Shamit Kachru (Executive Director, Quantitative Research, PDT Partners). Attendees will also have after-hours access to the Museum’s Hall of Human Life and the new Making Waves exhibit. This event will be 6:00-8:30pm. 

If you are not attending the Summer School (August 3-7), but would like to attend this event, [please RSVP here](https://app.smartsheet.com/b/form/019f56b65e607f61add532b83fca44c4).

Next, we are hosting our first IAIFI Industry Day on Friday, August 14 at the MIT Samberg Conference Center. The event aims to foster collaboration, awareness, and networking between IAIFI industry partners, IAIFI researchers, and attendees of the IAIFI Summer Workshop. It will include flash talks from industry partners, a catered networking lunch, and an expo portion. [Read more about the event here](https://iaifi.org/industry-day), including the list of participating of industry partners. This event will be 11:00am-3:45pm.

[RSVP here for Industry Day.](https://app.smartsheet.com/b/form/2f20ada38f024577809106ad08188294)
