## News from IAIFI Management
 
### Special ESRC Colloquium this Friday, November 13, 2026

This Friday, November 13, 2026 at 2:00 pm IAIFI's' Ethical and Social Responsibility Committee will host a special colloquium with Deep Deka (Principal Research Scientist at the MIT Energy Initiative) on AI and the energy grid. See details below. There will be no pre-colloquium lunch this week. 

### IAIFI Fall Social next Friday, November 20, 2026 at 5:00 pm

Our IAIFI Fall Social will be held on Friday, November 20, 2026 at 5:00 pm at Sacco's/American Flatbread in Davis Square. Food and beverages will be provided and candlepin bowling will be available! Kids welcome. Please [RSVP here](https://app.smartsheet.com/b/form/01a1c11b1425707da44736b09b3565c1).

### No Lunch Discussion This Week

There will be no IAIFI Lunch Discussion this week. The Coffee Hour will be held at 1:00 pm as usual. You can still [sign up to lead a lunch discussion this semester here](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSf6_sXJqBoVirNvf_H7lo6OkyxEHNwjBBuOc-XBkWWJcXN36g/viewform).
