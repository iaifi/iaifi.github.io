### RSVP for IAIFI Speed Networking on Friday, October 16th!

This Friday, October 16, 2026 at 2:00 pm in the MIT Kolker Room, we will host an IAIFI speed networking session! This will include several rounds of small group networking (we will provide some guiding questions for you). If there is enough interest, we will also include a virtual option (to network with other virtual participants).
 
We will provide pizza and other refreshments. [Please RSVP](https://app.smartsheet.com/b/form/01a1356ff7e67d6b9e2ceb26a547e338) so we know how much pizza to order.
 
The speed networking will be followed by our usual unstructured networking in the IAIFI Penthouse at 3:00 pm.
