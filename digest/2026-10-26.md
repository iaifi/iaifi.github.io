### Welcome new IAIFI Project Coordinator, Maria Figueiredo!

<img class="image" src="images/small-photo-maria-figueiredo" align="right" style="max-width:226px;width:{{photo_size}}%" hspace="10" vspace="10"/>We are pleased to welcome our new IAIFI Project Coordinator, Maria Figueiredo! She has extensive event and project coordination experience that we are sure IAIFI will benefit from. Please be sure to say hi to Maria and welcome her to IAIFI! 

### IAIFI Forum Meetings

The first IAIFI Forum meetings of the semester have begun (more details below). Anyone in IAIFI is welcome to attend these meetings to provide input on IAIFI activities in Research, Talent, and Community, respectively. If you are interested in joining the Board of any of these Forums, email [iaifi@mit.edu](mailto:iaifi@mit.edu).

* Community Forum: Met on Friday, October 23, 2026, 1:00–1:30 pm
* Research Forum: Monday, October 26, 2026, 11:00 am–12:00 pm
* Talent Forum: Wednesday, October 28, 2026, 2:00–3:00 pm
