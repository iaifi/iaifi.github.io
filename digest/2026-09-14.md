### Reminder: Junior Investigator Participation Form due Friday, September 18, 2026

Please be sure to complete your [IAIFI Participation Survey](https://app.smartsheet.com/b/form/01a07eb464ff73688c5ac65229e9e169) by this Friday, September 18, 2026. IAIFI Junior Investigators are required to complete the form, but all IAIFI members are welcome to fill it out & provide input. Filling out this form should take less than 10 minutes and helps our organizing teams ensure our activities and events are as beneficial as possible to the IAIFI community!

### Sign up to present a poster at the IAIFI Mini-Symposium Poster Session on September 18, 2026

On Friday, September 18, 2026, we plan to hold an IAIFI mini-symposium, featuring lightning talks from IAIFI Senior Investigators and Affiliates to present their research interests at 2:00 pm, followed by a poster session for IAIFI postdocs and grad students to present their research at 3:00 pm. [Sign up here](https://app.smartsheet.com/b/form/01a07ed1fa14769baba69621a3775138) to present a poster. 

### Update: IAIFI Summer Workshop Recordings Available on YouTube
We have begun posting [recordings from the 2026 IAIFI Summer Workshop](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLTWir0mD34MA) to our [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@iaifiinstituteforaifundame3333)! We will continue adding them as we obtain permission from the speakers, but there are several videos available now!
