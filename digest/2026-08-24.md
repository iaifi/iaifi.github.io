### Lunch Discussion Sign-Ups

Sign-ups are open for IAIFI Lunch Discussions for Fall 2026! These events will be **Thursdays at 12:00pm**, at the IAIFI Penthouse and on Zoom, with lunch provided. The Lunch Discussions are a great opportunity to share your own research, discuss larger-scale themes at the intersection of AI+Physics, and/or spark new ideas and collaborations. The discussions will also be followed by the weekly IAIFI Coffee Hour on Thursdays at 1:00 pm.

Fill out the form below to volunteer to lead a discussion, or to suggest a discussion topic for a future event! [Past events](https://iaifi.org/lunch-discussions.html) have ranged from people presenting on their own research, to open-ended discussions such as *AI+Science Unpopular Opinions* and *How do LLMs impact work for students & postdocs?*

[Lunch Discussion Form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSf6_sXJqBoVirNvf_H7lo6OkyxEHNwjBBuOc-XBkWWJcXN36g/viewform){:.button.button--outline-primary.button--pill.button--sm}

### Opportunity for IAIFI Junior Investigators at Summit for AI Institutes Leadership (SAIL)

Each year, the NSF AI Institutes host a “Summit for AI Institute Leadership (SAIL),” which brings together researchers from all of the National AI Institutes to share what they are working on and discuss management of the Institutes. For the second time, they are planning to host a graduate student and postdoc workshop this year, and each Institute is able to invite up to two junior researchers, paid for by the Summit organizers. We are seeking volunteers who are interested in representing IAIFI at the workshop. 

The Summit and workshop will take place in San Diego, November 2-4, 2026. As a participant, in addition to participating in the workshop, you would have the opportunity to present a poster to SAIL attendees and interact with AI Institute leaders. **Check out more details at the link below.**

If you are interested, **email iaifi@mit.edu by end of day today, August 24**. If more than two people are interested, we will select two representatives from the volunteers.
