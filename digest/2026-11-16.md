## News from IAIFI Management
 
### Colloquium this Friday, November 20, 2026

This Friday, November 20, 2026 at 2:00 pm IAIFI will host a colloquium with Jake Nibauer, Postdoctoral Fellow, IAIFI and Harvard/Smithsonian Center for Astrophysics ITC on Dark Matter, Dynamics, and Derivatives: Probing Sub-Galactic Structure with Stellar Streams. See details below. There will be no pre-colloquium lunch this week. 

### IAIFI Fall Social this Friday, November 20, 2026 at 5:00 pm

Our IAIFI Fall Social will be held on Friday, November 20, 2026 at 5:00 pm at Sacco's/American Flatbread in Davis Square. Food and beverages will be provided and candlepin bowling will be available! Kids welcome. Please [RSVP here](https://app.smartsheet.com/b/form/01a1c11b1425707da44736b09b3565c1).
