### Reminder: IAIFI Colloquium and Lunch with Speaker this Friday, October 23, 2026!

Our Fall 2026 Colloquium series continues this Friday, October 23, 2026 at 2:00 pm with a talk from IAIFI Fellow Nicole Hartman (details in “Events this Week” below)! Before the talk, IAIFI students and postdocs are invited to an informal, catered networking lunch with Nicole at 12:30pm in the Penthouse (26-648). Lunch and refreshments will be provided.

### IAIFI Forum Meetings

The first IAIFI Forum meetings of the semester will begin this week and next (more details below). Anyone in IAIFI is welcome to attend these meetings to provide input on IAIFI activities in Research, Talent, and Community, respectively. If you are interested in joining the Board of any of these Forums, email [iaifi@mit.edu](mailto:iaifi@mit.edu).

* Community Forum: Friday, October 23, 2026, 1:00–1:30 pm
* Research Forum: Monday, October 26, 2026, 11:00 am–12:00 pm
* Talent Forum: Wednesday, October 28, 2026, 2:00–3:00 pm
