### Junior Investigator Participation Form due Friday, September 18, 2026

Looking toward Fall 2026, we are sending out our [next semesterly IAIFI Participation Survey](https://app.smartsheet.com/b/form/01a07eb464ff73688c5ac65229e9e169). In the form, you can reflect on your level of participation this past semester, indicate your expected participation in Fall 2026, and provide input on IAIFI broadly. The form should take less than 10 minutes to complete.
 
IAIFI Junior Investigators are required to complete the form, but all IAIFI members are welcome to fill it out & provide input. Filling out this form helps our organizing teams ensure our activities and events are as beneficial as possible to the IAIFI community!
 
The deadline for responses will be **Friday, September 18, 2026**, but we encourage you to respond as soon as possible.

### Save the Date: IAIFI Mini-Symposium on September 18, 2026

On Friday, September 18, 2026, we plan to hold an IAIFI mini-symposium, featuring lightning talks from IAIFI Senior Investigators and Affiliates to present their research interests at 2:00 pm, followed by a poster session for IAIFI postdocs and grad students to present their research at 3:00 pm. [Sign up here](https://app.smartsheet.com/b/form/01a07ed1fa14769baba69621a3775138) to present a poster. 

### Update: Fall 2026 IAIFI Coffee Hour will be held Thursdays at 1:00 pm starting this week!
The coffee hours will follow the lunch discussions, which will be held on Thursdays at 12:00 pm beginning September 25, 2026. We are also accepting [submissions to lead a lunch discussion](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSf6_sXJqBoVirNvf_H7lo6OkyxEHNwjBBuOc-XBkWWJcXN36g/viewform)!
