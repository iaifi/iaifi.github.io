### Reminder: First IAIFI Colloquium this Friday, September 25, 2026!

We will kick off our Fall 2026 Colloquium series this Friday, September 25, 2026 at 2:00 pm with a talk from IAIFI Fellow Adrian Bayer (details in "Events this Week" below)! Before the talk, IAIFI students and postdocs are invited to an informal, catered networking lunch with Adrian. Lunch and refreshments will be provided. The event will be at 12:30pm on Friday, September 25, at the Penthouse (26-648). 

### Sign up to volunteer at the Cambridge Science Carnival

We are looking for volunteers to participate in our booth at the [Cambridge Science Carnival](https://cambridgesciencecarnival.org) on Sunday, October 4, 2026. This is a fun opportunity to engage with the public on AI+Physics. If you are interested in volunteering for a shift, please [sign up here](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1Lypv0omjMcZ0e_MoKwA-zVPvOvH6aTOA7dvaU3b-9MQ/edit?usp=sharing) by Monday, September 28.
