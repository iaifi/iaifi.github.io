### IAIFI Industry Day (Friday, August 14)

We are hosting our first IAIFI Industry Day on Friday, August 14 at the MIT Samberg Conference Center. The event aims to foster collaboration, awareness, and networking between IAIFI industry partners, IAIFI researchers, and attendees of the IAIFI Summer Workshop. It will include brief flash talks from industry partners, a catered networking lunch, and an expo portion. You can [read more about the event here](https://iaifi.org/industry-day), including the list of participating of industry partners. [RSVP required.](https://app.smartsheet.com/b/form/2f20ada38f024577809106ad08188294)

### No Coffee Hour This Week
Please note, per the Events Calendar, there will be no IAIFI Coffee Hour this week or next. These will resume on **Thursday, August 20, 2026**.
